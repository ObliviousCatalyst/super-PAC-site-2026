The main problem in America is equality for everything and everyone. The legacy party's main goal was to make a change for the better, to ensure our country is safer and more equal. Something that they would focus on is “Bringing wealth and power back to the common man. Matan Moshe, the president of the party has clearly stated, “We are very big on anti-Monopoly and anti-incorporations in favor of workers and everyone else in the lower tax bracket.” Explaining how we need to change the way money is used in America so that people are able to obtain and spend money equally.

The legacy party's conclusion for their goal was to make changes that would lead America into the path all people can enjoy. They wanted to learn from the country's mistakes to build a brighter future, so that such mistakes won't happen again. Matan Moshe explained himself, “Our party stands for lots and lots of change. We think that what we have now in America is not working, it hasn't worked, it won't work” the message rings true for all Americans. This regime and its capitalist policies don't work and never did.

Another pillar of the party was safety. For a long time America couldn't keep it in its pants when it came to foreign policy. Many politicians of yesteryear opted to force themselves into the issues of both their allies and enemies rather than finding solutions to their own. So Mr. Moshe had an idea: “to make tighter borders around the US and withdraw from foreign affairs until we could find solid ground to stand on ourselves.” The policies of the Legacy party will make sure that America will provide aid for other countries in their time of need. However, before we have to take care of others, we also have problems to face for our own. Because if we are unable to help our struggles, how can we expect to help others?

However The legacy party despite matan's best efforts fell behind the other parties in the polls therefore resulting in them not being able to debate, Thus leaving them in an awkward position in which, due to the flawed election system of our country, their continued presence in the political race would only serve to help the parties they least agreed with. And so with a heavy heart, our candidate Matan went onstage to announce that he and the legacy party would be dropping out of the race and subsequently entrust their power and legacy to the social party.

That brings us to... us. We have also been left in an awkward situation, in which the party we have been backing for the entire election season no longer exists, and they have left their faith in another party which we were known critics of up to this point. As a super PAC we do not simply get to drop out of the race. We are obligated by all of our supporters to see this through.
